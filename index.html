About

From shift-left automation to agentic AI architecture

I'm an AI Evaluation Architect and Agentic QA & Reliability leader with 18+ years designing scalable test architectures, driving AI-first quality initiatives, and leading QA teams across enterprise SaaS and cloud environments — spanning Oracle, Cisco (AppDynamics), Liberty Wireless Technologies, and Chargebee.

My hands-on practice runs from functional test automation framework design and test-early / shift-left strategy to one-click performance benchmarking and building Quality Centers of Excellence. Most recently at Chargebee, I built and led a 10-member performance test team from scratch and led that discipline into agentic AI — architecting Test Pilot (60% faster QA cycles) and ATPAR (performance testing cut from ~3 days to 1 hour). Over the last year I've extended this further into LLM and agent evaluation, and I teach it hands-on at SharadhaAI, the Agentic AI training program I run alongside my own projects.

Based in India. Open to Staff Engineer / QA Architect / Engineering Leadership roles in Agentic AI Evaluation & Quality, and to AI evaluation & governance consulting.
